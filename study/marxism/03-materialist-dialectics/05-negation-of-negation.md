The unity of opposites tells us why things develop (Lesson 3.3), and the law of
quantity and quality how they develop, gradually and by leaps (Lesson 3.4). The third
basic law answers the last question: **where is development going, and along what
road?** The course's answer is that development moves forward overall, but not in a
straight line: it goes through negation and the negation of that negation, seems to
come back to where it started, but at a higher level, like a spiral staircase. This
{{term law of the negation of the negation | 否定之否定规律}} reveals the **direction and
path of development** (世界发展的方向和道路). It also gives one of the most useful
attitudes in the course, toward the past, toward tradition and toward other cultures:
neither keep everything nor throw everything away.

## Does development follow a pattern?

The lecture opened with three old Chinese sayings, three different answers to whether
the world's development has a pattern:

| Saying | Source | View of development |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 穷则变，变则通，通则久 (when things reach an end they change; change opens a way through; a way through lasts) | *Book of Changes*, 《周易·系辞下》 | Things change, and change is how they keep going: close to dialectics |
| 道之大原出于天，天不变，道亦不变 (the great source of the Way is Heaven; Heaven does not change, so the Way does not change either) | Dong Zhongshu (董仲舒), Han dynasty | Nothing fundamental ever changes: a metaphysical view |
| 话说天下大势，分久必合，合久必分 (the empire, long divided, must unite; long united, must divide) | Opening line of *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* 《三国演义》 | History goes round in circles: a cyclical view |

The slides give no sources; they are added here. Materialist dialectics agrees with
none of the last two: the world does change (against Dong Zhongshu), and it does not
simply go round in circles (against the cyclical view). It does have a pattern, and
that pattern starts with affirmation and negation.

## Affirmation and negation

Every thing holds two tendencies inside it: one that keeps it going and one that pushes
it toward its end. A company has what keeps it profitable (its products, its customers)
and what threatens it (ageing technology, rising costs). A student's habits include
those that keep their grades up and those that drag them down.

::: define Affirmation and negation | 肯定与否定
The two opposite aspects or tendencies inside every thing. The **affirming factor**
(肯定因素) is what keeps the existing thing in existence; the **negating factor**
(否定因素) is what pushes the existing thing toward its end.

> 肯定因素是维持现存事物存在的因素，否定因素是促使现存事物灭亡的因素。
:::

Affirmation and negation are a pair of opposites, so everything Lesson 3.3 says about
contradiction applies to them:

- **Opposition.** They are two opposite sides and tendencies. While the affirming side
  dominates, the thing keeps its quality and goes on existing; once the negating side
  gains the upper hand, the thing is negated: it turns into something else.
- **Unity.** They depend on each other, contain each other and define each other, and
  under certain conditions turn into each other. There is no "keeping going" without
  something that threatens to end it, and the new thing that the negation produces has
  its own affirming side.

## The dialectical view of negation

What does it mean for something to be "negated"? In everyday talk, negating means
denying or destroying. Materialist dialectics means something richer.

::: define Dialectical negation | 辩证否定
Negation as materialist dialectics understands it:

1. it is the thing's **self-negation**, its self-development, the result of the movement
   of its own inner contradiction (否定是事物的自我否定、自我发展，是事物内部矛盾运动的结果);
2. it is a **link in development**: the turning of the old thing into a new one, a leap
   from the old quality to the new (否定是事物发展的环节，是旧事物向新事物的转变，是旧质到
   新质的飞跃);
3. it is a **link connecting old and new**: the new grows out of the old and is joined to
   it through the negation (否定是新旧事物联系的环节);
4. its essence is **sublation** (辩证否定的实质是"扬弃").
:::

::: define Sublation | 扬弃
The essence of dialectical negation: the new thing both **criticises and inherits** the
old one, both **overcomes** its negative elements and **keeps** its positive ones.

> 扬弃，即新事物对旧事物既批判又继承，既克服又保留。
:::

The Chinese word 扬弃 translates Hegel's German *Aufheben*, which means at the same time
"to cancel", "to preserve" and "to lift up". All three are in the idea: the old form is
cancelled, its useful content is preserved, and both are raised to a higher level.

Engels gave the classic example when he explained how Marx and he dealt with Hegel.
Feuerbach, he wrote in *Ludwig Feuerbach and the End of Classical German Philosophy*
(《路德维希·费尔巴哈和德国古典哲学的终结》, 1886), "broke through" Hegel's system and simply
threw it aside. But simply declaring a philosophy wrong does not overcome it, least of
all a work with as much influence as Hegel's. It had to be "sublated" in its proper
sense: its **form** criticised and destroyed, but the **new content** won through that
form rescued. That is what Marx did: he kept Hegel's dialectics (the rational kernel)
and threw away its idealist shell (Lesson 3.1).

Marx put the critical spirit of this view into one famous sentence, in the afterword to
the second edition of *Capital*, volume 1 (1873): dialectics, in its positive
understanding of what exists, includes at the same time the understanding of its
negation, of its inevitable passing away; it regards every form in its movement, and so
in its transient side; "辩证法不崇拜任何东西，按其本质来说，它是批判的和革命的"
(dialectics lets nothing impose on it, and is in its essence critical and
revolutionary). Lesson 3.6 returns to this essence.

| Aspect | Metaphysical view of negation | Dialectical view of negation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where negation comes from | From outside: something destroys the thing | From inside: the thing's own contradiction |
| What negation is | Pure denial, destruction, an end | A link in development, a leap to a new quality |
| Old and new | Cut off from each other, a clean break | Connected through the negation |
| What is kept | Nothing ("affirm everything" or "negate everything") | The positive elements: sublation |

::: callout pitfall Negation is sublation, not throwing away
The commonest mistake is to read "negation" as "rejection". A dialectical negation
always **keeps** something: the plant keeps the grain's life, socialism keeps the
productive forces built under capitalism, Marx kept Hegel's dialectics. A pure
rejection that keeps nothing (or an acceptance that changes nothing) is exactly what
the dialectical view of negation rules out.
:::

### What the dialectical view of negation asks of us

1. Take a **scientific, analytical attitude** to everything: grasp both its present
   state and its direction of development (对待一切事物都要采取科学分析的态度，既要把握住
   它的现存状态，又要把握住它的发展趋向).
2. **Oppose simply affirming everything or negating everything** (反对简单地肯定一切或否定
   一切).

A slide (not shown in class) applies this to culture:

| Toward | Do | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| China's fine traditional culture (中华优秀传统文化) | Value its transmission (注重传承); transform it creatively and develop it innovatively (创造性转化，创新性发展) | Neither worship the past wholesale nor throw it away: keep what is alive and renew its form |
| Other peoples' cultural achievements | Neither copy them wholesale nor reject them (不照搬，不排斥); absorb them critically and make them serve our needs (批判吸收，为我所用) | Neither "total westernisation" nor closing the door |

## The negation of the negation

One negation is not the end of the story. The thing produced by a negation is itself
one-sided, and it contains its own negating factor, so in time it is negated too.

::: law The law of the negation of the negation | 否定之否定规律
A thing develops, through its inner contradiction between affirmation and negation, in
a cycle of **affirmation → negation → negation of the negation** (肯定—否定—否定之否定).
The **first negation** resolves the contradiction only preliminarily: the thing in the
stage of negation is still one-sided (第一次否定使矛盾得到初步解决，而处于否定阶段的事物
仍然具有片面性). The **second negation** achieves the unity of the opposites and resolves
the contradiction fundamentally (第二次否定实现了对立面的统一，使矛盾得到根本解决). The
result seems to return to the starting point, but on a higher level: development as a
whole moves forward **like waves** and rises **like a spiral** (波浪式前进、螺旋式上升).
:::

The three stages are often called thesis, antithesis and synthesis after Hegel's
followers, but the course's words are 肯定, 否定 and 否定之否定. Look at what each stage
does:

- **Affirmation**: the thing as it is, with both tendencies in it, the affirming one
  dominant.
- **Negation**: the negating side wins; the thing becomes its opposite. It overcomes
  the one-sidedness of the first stage but has its own one-sidedness.
- **Negation of the negation**: the second negation overcomes that one-sidedness too
  and joins what was positive in both earlier stages. It looks like a return to the
  start ("回复"), because features of the first stage reappear, but it is richer.

### Engels' two examples

Engels gave the classic illustrations in *Anti-Dühring* (《反杜林论》, 1878), in the
chapter on the negation of the negation.

::: example A grain of barley
Engels: if a grain of barley falls on suitable soil, under the influence of heat and
moisture it germinates; the grain as such disappears, is negated, and in its place is
the plant that grows from it. The plant grows, flowers, is fertilised and finally
produces grains of barley again; as soon as they are ripe the stalk dies, is negated in
its turn. Follow the three stages.
== Affirmation ==
The grain of barley (麦粒).
== Negation ==
The plant (植株): the grain is gone, "negated" by what grew out of it. But the plant
keeps the grain's life: this is a negation that preserves, a sublation.
== Negation of the negation ==
New grains of barley (新的麦粒): the plant dies, negated in turn, and the result is
again barley grain, "not one, but ten, twenty or thirty fold".
== What it shows ==
A seeming return to the start (grain again), but on a higher level (many grains, and in
nature also often improved varieties): development as a spiral.
:::

::: callout note Where the barley-grain passage comes from
The slide attributes the barley-grain passage to *Dialectics of Nature* (《自然辩证法》). It
is from *Anti-Dühring* (Part One, chapter XIII, "Dialectics: Negation of the
Negation"), the same chapter as the next example, which the slide attributes
correctly.
:::

::: example Common ownership of land
Engels: all civilised peoples began with common ownership of the land. As agriculture
developed, that ownership became a fetter on production; it was abolished, negated, and
after longer or shorter intermediate stages turned into private property. But at the
higher stage of agriculture that private property itself brought about, private
property in turn became a fetter on production, so the demand arises to negate it too
and turn it back into common property. Follow the stages.
== Affirmation ==
Primitive common ownership (原始公有制).
== Negation ==
Private ownership (私有制): it freed production at first, then became its fetter.
== Negation of the negation ==
Communist common ownership (共产主义公有制). Engels stresses that this demand is **not**
to restore primitive common ownership, but to set up a "far higher and more developed"
form of common possession, which far from hampering production will free it and let
modern chemical discoveries and mechanical inventions be fully used.
== What it shows ==
"Common" returns, but on the basis of everything private ownership developed: the
course's account of the direction of social development.
:::

::: callout pitfall The spiral is not a circle
"It comes back to the start" does **not** mean history goes round in circles, as in
"分久必合，合久必分". In a circle you return to the same point; in a spiral you return
to a point *above* it. The cyclical view (历史循环论) misses the forward movement; a
straight-line view misses the return and the twists. Nor is the law a template that
forces every process into exactly three steps: it describes the direction and path that
development shows as a whole.
:::

## Progress and twists

::: law Development is the unity of progress and twists | 前进性与曲折性的统一
The law of the negation of the negation reveals that development is the unity of
**progressiveness** (前进性) and **tortuousness** (曲折性).

- **Progressiveness**: each negation is a qualitative change that pushes the thing to a
  new stage; and each cycle is open: the end of one cycle is the start of the next
  (前进性体现在每一次否定都是质变，都把事物推进到新阶段上；每一个周期都是开放的，前一个周期
  的终点是下一个周期的起点).
- **Tortuousness**: shown in the **return** (回复性); there are temporary halts and even
  setbacks (曲折性体现在回复性上，其中有暂时的停顿甚至倒退).

So development does not go forward in a straight line, but rises in a spiral.
:::

**Methodological requirement.** Understand that the development of human society is the
dialectical unity of progress and twists: on the one hand, be firmly convinced that
history is a process of continuous development, and never lose the confidence and
courage to push forward; on the other, take the twists along the way seriously, and
drive development forward by meeting challenges and resolving contradictions (要深刻
认识人类社会历史发展是前进性与曲折性的辩证统一，既要坚信社会历史是不断发展的进程，任何时候都
不能失去开拓前进的信心和勇气，同时又要高度重视和认真对待前进中的曲折，在应对挑战和解决矛盾中
推动社会发展).

In short: **the future is bright, the road is winding** (前途是光明的，道路是曲折的). Both
halves are needed. Seeing only the bright future breeds blind optimism that is crushed
by the first setback; seeing only the winding road breeds pessimism.

## Think it through: an example of the negation of the negation

::: example Think it through: give an example that confirms the negation of the negation
The week-4 question: 举出一个能印证"否定之否定"原理的例子并解释. The slide's own suggestions
were studying, the cycle from sleep to waking, and iterating a work plan. Here is a
classic from the history of science, the theory of light.
== Affirmation ==
Newton (17th century) held the **particle** theory: light is a stream of tiny
corpuscles. It explained reflection and the straight-line travel of light.
== Negation ==
In the 19th century, Young's double-slit experiment (interference) and Fresnel's work
on diffraction, then Maxwell's electromagnetic theory, established the **wave**
theory: light is an electromagnetic wave. The particle view was negated, but what it
had explained was kept: the wave theory also accounts for reflection and straight-line
travel. A sublation, not a mere rejection.
== Negation of the negation ==
The wave theory could not explain the photoelectric effect. Einstein (1905) proposed
light quanta (photons), and quantum theory arrived at **wave–particle duality**: light
is both. "Particles" are back, but not Newton's particles: the new view keeps what was
right in both earlier stages and unites the two opposites at a higher level.
== Why it fits the law ==
Each negation came from the theory's own contradiction with experiment (self-negation);
each kept the positive content of what it negated (sublation); the third stage looks
like a return to the first (回复性) but is richer (前进性); and the path had long pauses
and disputes (曲折性).
== Model answer ==
否定之否定规律揭示了事物发展是经过肯定—否定—否定之否定的过程，是前进性与曲折性的统一，
呈现波浪式前进、螺旋式上升的趋势。以人类对光的本质的认识为例：牛顿提出光的微粒说（肯定）；
托马斯·杨的双缝干涉实验和麦克斯韦电磁理论确立了光的波动说，否定了微粒说，但保留了微粒说
对反射、直线传播的合理解释（第一次否定，是扬弃）；爱因斯坦提出光量子说，现代物理学确立了光的
波粒二象性，既克服了两种学说各自的片面性，又保留了它们的合理成分，表面上"回到"微粒说，实际上
在更高的基础上实现了对立面的统一（否定之否定）。可见，认识的发展不是直线式的，而是螺旋式
上升的。因此我们既要对发展前途充满信心，又要正确对待前进中的曲折。
:::

The slide's everyday answers work the same way. **Studying**: you know something
roughly (affirmation), discover what you don't understand (negation), and understand it
again at a deeper level (negation of the negation). **Iterating a plan**: draft,
criticism, a revised plan that keeps what worked. The cycle from sleep to waking is
weaker as an example: it repeats without rising, more a circle than a spiral, so if you
use it, say what is new at each return.

## The three laws together

The lecture closed with a map of all three basic laws:

| Law | Question it answers | Its contents | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unity of opposites (对立统一规律) | The **source and driving force** of the world's development | Identity and struggle of contradiction and their roles in development; universality and particularity of contradiction and how they are related | 3.3 |
| Quantitative and qualitative change (量变质变规律) | The **state** in which the world exists and the **form** of development | Things as the unity of quality, quantity and measure; quantitative and qualitative change | 3.4 |
| Negation of the negation (否定之否定规律) | The **direction and path** of the world's development | The interaction of affirmation and negation; the completion of dialectical negation | 3.5 |

::: callout key The one thing to remember
Dialectical negation is **sublation**: keep the good, overcome the bad. Done twice, it
makes development a **spiral**: forward overall (前进性) by way of returns and setbacks
(曲折性). Hence: confidence in the future, seriousness about the twists.
:::

::: callout exam How this law is examined
- **Form vs content**: the opposition of affirmation and negation is the *content*;
  "spiral ascent / wave-like advance" is its *form of appearance*. The in-class question
  below turns on exactly this, and "endless cycle" is always the metaphysical trap.
- **辨析题**: "negation is a clean break" (wrong), "development is a straight line"
  (wrong), "the negation of the negation is a return to the starting point" (wrong:
  a return at a higher level).
- **Material analysis**: a historical case with setbacks: answer with the unity of
  progressiveness and tortuousness and its methodology.
- **Culture**: attitudes to tradition and foreign culture are sublation: 创造性转化、
  创新性发展; 批判吸收，为我所用.
:::

Methodology of the dialectical view of negation :: Analyse things scientifically, their present state and direction; oppose affirming everything or negating everything.
Attitude to fine traditional culture :: Value its transmission; creative transformation and innovative development (创造性转化，创新性发展).
Attitude to other peoples' culture :: Neither copy nor reject; absorb critically and make it serve us (不照搬，不排斥；批判吸收，为我所用).
The cycle of the negation of the negation :: Affirmation → negation → negation of the negation (肯定—否定—否定之否定).
First negation vs second negation :: The first resolves the contradiction preliminarily but stays one-sided; the second unites the opposites and resolves it fundamentally.
Form of development by the negation of the negation :: Wave-like advance, spiral ascent (波浪式前进、螺旋式上升).
Progressiveness (前进性) :: Each negation is a qualitative change to a new stage; each cycle is open, its end the next one's start.
Tortuousness (曲折性) :: Shown in the return (回复性): temporary halts and even setbacks.
Methodology of the law of the negation of the negation :: Be confident that history keeps developing; take the twists seriously and push development forward by resolving contradictions.
Engels' barley grain :: Grain → plant → many new grains (*Anti-Dühring*): the negation of the negation in nature.
Engels' land ownership :: Primitive common ownership → private ownership → a far higher form of common ownership (*Anti-Dühring*).
What the law of the negation of the negation reveals :: The direction and path of development.
