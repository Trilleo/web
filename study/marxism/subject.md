马克思主义基本原理 is a compulsory course at Chinese universities. It sets out Marxism as
the textbook presents it: what it is and how Marx and Engels founded it, then its
philosophy — **dialectical materialism**. The first half asks what the world is made of
(matter, and how consciousness relates to it) and how it changes (connection,
development, and the three laws of dialectics). The second half turns to knowledge:
practice, how knowing works, and what truth is.

The lessons explain the course's ideas in plain English, in the course's own terms, with
the Chinese term beside each key concept so they match the slides and the exam. Each one
has the definitions and principles to learn (with the "methodological requirement" that
follows from each), the thinkers and dates behind them, worked cases, the teacher's
weekly discussion questions with model answers, flashcards and a quiz.

The course is still being taught: lessons follow the lectures up to 16 October 2026, and
new ones are added as the term goes on.
