This course has two halves. The **analogue** half follows a signal through real
circuits: how semiconductors conduct, how diodes and transistors behave, how a
transistor amplifies, and how op-amps filter signals, generate waveforms and turn
mains AC into a steady DC supply. The **digital** half starts again from zero:
numbers in binary, the algebra of 0 and 1, and circuits built from logic gates.

Every lesson starts from the idea, then the rules, then worked examples with real
numbers. Try the circuits and graphs — change a value and see what happens — and
finish with the quiz. Chinese terms sit beside the English ones, so the lessons
match the slides and the exam.

The course is still being taught: lessons follow the lectures up to 15 October 2026,
and new ones are added as the term goes on.
